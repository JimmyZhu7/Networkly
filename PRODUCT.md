# Networkly

## Platform
Web. Django templates, htmx, vendored JavaScript, and self-hosted fonts. PostgreSQL owns application data. No frontend framework migration is needed.

## Audience and job
Students managing campus recruiting, especially finance and consulting, across multiple markets. Their recurring task is to find a role, identify people at that firm, record conversations, and act on the next follow-up or deadline.

## Product truth
The public opportunities feed is free. Roles remain grouped by firm. Each student's contacts, application stages, conversations, and settings are private. Today derives an actionable queue from those records. Calendar joins scheduled conversations with recruiting dates. The assistant reasons over the student's own records. Gmail connection and automated capture are optional, separate from sign-in.

## Redesign mandate
The founder requested a decisive, comprehensive visual refurbishment on 2026-09-05: beautiful, harmonious, very simple, and fully functional. Build directly in the existing application. Keep factual distinctions, uncertainty, working actions, tenant boundaries, and existing URLs. The founder has delegated visual and presentation decisions for this pass.

## Success
A first-time student can identify the current page, find a role, save it, find a contact, record an interaction, and see upcoming activity without interpreting unfamiliar visual conventions. Desktop, mobile, keyboard, light theme, and dark theme remain usable.
