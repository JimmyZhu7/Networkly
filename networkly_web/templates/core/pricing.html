A Few Good Questions

Do I Need an Account to See Opportunities?

No. The feed is public. An account adds the CRM: the Today queue and the Network board.

Will the Free Plan Shrink When Paid Tiers Launch?

No. This list is the floor. Free may grow; it will not shrink. Pro is for sync, speed, and heavier AI use, the things that cost real money to run.

What Would a Team Plan Look Like for My Club?

Shared tiers, shared contacts, one flat plan an officer manages, not per-seat billing to negotiate. If you run a finance society, we want your input before it ships.

What Happens to My Data If I Stop Using Networkly?

Export it as CSV whenever you like. Delete your account yourself and everything goes with it, immediately.
