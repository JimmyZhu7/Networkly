{% comment %} ====================================================================== WHAT HAPPENS AFTER A CARD LEAVES THE QUEUE. Every quick action on this page (Snooze, Skip, Done, They replied) is an htmx POST that swaps the whole of `#today-cockpit`'s innerHTML. Two things were wrong with the moment right after that swap, and both are handled here rather than in `crm/_cockpit.html` — the container survives the swap, its contents do not, so the listeners belong on the survivor. 1. FOCUS FELL ON THE FLOOR. The button that was focused when you pressed it is removed by the swap, and a removed element's focus goes to ``. For a keyboard or screen-reader user that means every single action on this page ends by dumping them back at the top of the document, to tab down through the header, the ribbon, the brief and the situation strip before reaching the queue they never left. Nine acts in a morning is nine round trips. So: remember where focus was, and after the swap put it on the same position in the same lane — the next card's first action, since the card that was there is exactly the one that just went away. If the lane emptied, the lane's own heading takes it (`tabindex="-1"` is added here, not in the markup, because it is only ever a programmatic target and must not join the tab order). If the lane itself is gone, the cockpit takes it. Focus never lands nowhere. 2. THE COUNTS FLIPPED. The cards animate — a leaving card fades, a surviving one glides, all of it the browser's own view transition — and then "5 of 9 today" changed to "4 of 9 today" between two frames with nothing to mark it. Motion rule M4 (networkly.css §15b) says a number may move when it changes in response to the reader, and this is that case exactly: the reader pressed something and the number is the receipt. `chip-check` already exists for "a thing just became true"; the count borrows it for 200ms.
