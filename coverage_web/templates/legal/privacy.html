Privacy Policy

Your data, your choices, and how Networkly works.

Last updated 5 September 2026

On This PageYour DataConnectionsProvidersRetentionYour ControlsContact

The Short Version

  • Your recruiting workspace is private to your account. We do not sell your personal data.
  • Gmail and Google Calendar connections are optional. AI features send relevant information to Anthropic when you use them.
  • You can export your recruiting records, disconnect integrations, and delete your account from Settings.

Who Runs Networkly

Networkly is operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], [REGISTERED ADDRESS]. For privacy questions or requests, contact [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL].

What We Collect

We collect these categories of data.

  • Your account. Email address, and either a password (stored only as a one-way hash, never in readable form) or the identity your sign-in provider hands us. Plus what you tell us about yourself in Settings: name, school, class year, study level, recruiting cycle, target regions and tracks, work authorization, languages you work in, affiliations you list, timezone, and an optional profile picture.
  • Your recruiting data. Contacts (names, email addresses, firms, roles, your notes and angles), logged interactions, tasks, target firms and the tier you gave each, roles you are tracking, interview dates, calendar events, chat debriefs, fit scores, and the CSVs you import.
  • Your use of the product. Which pages and actions you use, as an event name and a small set of properties. We use these events to improve the product. Usage events do not include email bodies. Security systems also process technical information, such as IP addresses and failed sign-in attempts, to prevent abuse.
  • Your AI conversations, if you use them. If you use the assistant, the conversation is stored on your account so it is still there tomorrow, along with any facts the advisor was told to remember and any files you attached to a message.

Optional Mail Access

Mail access is optional and starts only after you connect Gmail through a separate Google consent screen. Google sign-in alone does not give Networkly access to your mail.

  • A Gmail account you connect. If Gmail Live is enabled on this deployment and you complete Google's consent screen, Networkly reads your Gmail with Google's read-only scope in order to log recruiting interactions automatically. This grants access to your mailbox, as described below.

For a connected mailbox, we store an encrypted refresh token, the mailbox address, and sync markers. Networkly reads the whole message, including its body and calendar attachments, and processes that text in memory to identify recruiting activity. We retain structured results such as the sender, reply status, and meeting details. Interactions may include a subject line or preview snippet of up to 300 characters. We do not retain complete messages through this sync.

Limited evidence retention. A mail fact, such as a stated departure or delivery failure, may retain one verbatim sentence of up to 500 characters. This quote supports the fact and is shown for your review. A delivery-failure quote can come from the mail server’s report. These retained excerpts are an exception to the rule against storing complete message bodies.

AI-assisted scans. When you press Scan Now, messages that cannot be classified deterministically may be checked by Anthropic. The request includes the subject line and Gmail preview snippet, not the full body or attachments. This is limited to one representative message from each of at most 100 unresolved threads per scan, and only runs when the AI service is enabled.

Disconnect Gmail in Settings to remove the stored connection token and stop future syncs. Networkly also attempts to revoke the Google grant. You can revoke it directly in your Google account. Disconnecting does not remove recruiting records already created; those remain until you delete them or your account.

Optional Calendar Access

If you connect Google Calendar, Networkly requests separate read-only permission to display calendar events in your workspace. It stores an encrypted connection token, sync markers, event titles, descriptions, locations, start and end times, and event identifiers. It does not create, edit, delete, or RSVP to events in Google Calendar.

Calendar and Gmail connections are separate. You can disconnect either from Settings without disconnecting the other. Imported events remain in Networkly until removed or your account is deleted. Calendar details may also be included as context when you use an AI feature that works with your schedule.

Google API Limited Use

Networkly's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google data is used to provide the connected recruiting and calendar features you choose. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train a general-purpose AI model. Relevant Gmail-derived information may be sent to Anthropic for the scan and Autopilot features described below. Operator access is limited to the cases allowed by Google’s policy, including your specific consent for support, security investigations, and legal obligations.

What We Use It For

We use your information to maintain your account, organize contacts and applications, log interactions, show your schedule, personalize recommendations, answer AI requests, and provide support. We also use technical and product events to secure and improve the service.

We do not use your recruiting data to train AI models or authorize that use by our providers. Anthropic’s commercial API has separate data-use terms; this is not a claim of zero retention by the provider.

Data Use Restrictions

  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • Your private recruiting records are scoped to your account. We do not make them visible to other users through the shared opportunity directory.
  • Aggregated delivery statistics may help assess whether mail to a firm’s domain tends to bounce. The shared statistic does not display your name or a contact’s email address.
  • We do not put email bodies in our logs.
  • We do not use third-party advertising trackers. Sign-in, connected services, and optional error monitoring involve the providers described below.

Who We Share Data With

We share data with these providers for the purposes described below.

  • Render hosts the app and the Postgres database. It processes stored account and recruiting information to operate the service.
  • Anthropic processes requests for enabled AI features:
    • The assistant, a coffee-chat brief, or a relationship summary: relevant profile and recruiting context, which can include contact names, firms, roles, notes, interactions, and schedule details. Context supplied automatically for these features excludes contact email addresses. Text you enter or files you attach can still contain personal information and are sent with your request.
    • Scan Now: the subject line and preview snippet for unresolved Gmail threads, as described in Optional Mail Access.
    • Autopilot: up to 200 pending contact cards per run, including each person’s name and email address, matched firm, subject-line or snippet evidence, and relevant stored mail facts. The model reviews whether a proposed card is supported by the evidence. Adding contacts still requires your action.
    These calls run only when the relevant feature is triggered and the AI service is configured.
  • Google receives a sign-in request if you sign in with Google, and provides mail or calendar data if you connect those services. The sign-in path asks for identity only and can never read mail. Gmail Live is a separate, second consent with its own credentials, and turning it down does not affect your ability to log in.
  • Your other sign-in provider, if the deployment offers Apple, Microsoft or LinkedIn and you choose one. Same identity-only scopes, same rule: no mailbox access.
  • An email provider, when one is configured, to deliver transactional mail such as password resets and deadline digests. It sees the address the mail is going to and what is in that mail.
  • A push service, if you turn on deadline alerts. Your browser's own push endpoint delivers the notification.
  • Sentry, if error monitoring is configured on the deployment, to report crashes. Default personal-information collection is disabled. Error reports may still contain technical request and diagnostic information.

We may also disclose data where the law actually requires it, or to protect someone's safety. If that ever happens and we are permitted to tell you, we will.

Cookies and Sessions

Networkly uses essential cookies for sign-in sessions and request-forgery protection. Authentication or short-lived status messages may also use cookies. Browser storage remembers preferences such as your theme. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels. Connected providers apply their own policies when you visit their consent or sign-in pages.

Retention

Your recruiting records are retained while your account is open, unless you delete them. Connection tokens are removed when you disconnect a service. Temporary sessions, rate-limit counters, and sync state follow their own operational expiry rules.

Gmail sync retains the structured records and limited evidence excerpts described above, rather than complete messages.

Account deletion removes your account and associated private database records from the active service. This does not erase data you exported, messages in your mailbox, or copies already held by connected providers. Hosting backups follow [BACKUP RETENTION PERIOD]; provider retention is governed by the applicable service arrangements.

Security

Production settings require HTTPS and secure cookies. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes. Gmail and Google Calendar refresh tokens are encrypted with a key held separately from the database. Account scoping and access controls help protect private records.

No service can guarantee absolute security. Report a suspected security issue to [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL].

If There Is a Breach

If personal data is exposed in a way likely to put you at risk, we will tell affected users directly, by email, without undue delay and in any case within 72 hours of confirming it, and we will notify any regulator the law requires us to notify. The notice will say what happened, what data was involved, and what you should do. We will not wait until we have a complete story to tell you there is a problem.

Your Rights

You do not have to file a request to exercise most of these. The tools are in the product.

  • Access and portability. Export your recruiting records as CSVs in a ZIP, including conversation text, advisor memories, and calendar events. The export page lists the included records. Passwords, connection secrets, the shared opportunity directory, and original uploaded files are not part of this record export.
  • Correction. Update your profile and editable recruiting records in Settings or on the record. Contact us if information cannot be corrected through those controls.
  • Deletion. Delete your account through Settings. The Retention section explains the scope of deletion and separately held copies.
  • Objection and restriction. Write to [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL]. You can also disconnect optional integrations and stop using AI features at any time.

Depending on where you live and which laws apply, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information; restrict or object to processing; withdraw consent; or complain to a data-protection authority. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We will not discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

We answer rights requests within 30 days. We will not charge you for one.

Age

Networkly is built for university students and is not intended for children. You need to be at least 16 to use it. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16, and if we learn we have, we delete the account and its data rather than asking for proof.

Where Your Data Lives

Where required, transfers must use an applicable legal safeguard. Contact us for details of the arrangements relevant to your data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the product changes. The date above identifies the revision. We will notify you in the app or by email before material changes take effect and obtain consent where required. A policy update does not remove rights you have under applicable law.
