Deleting your account immediately and permanently removes:

  • Your profile, your photo, and your account login
  • All of your contacts and their interaction history
  • Your target firms, tracked opportunities, and calendar events
  • Your captured email events, fit scores, tasks, and imports
  • Your assistant conversations, saved memories, and credit history
  • Your connected Google account, disconnected here and revoked at Google
  • Your notification subscriptions and product-usage events

Shared, aggregate-only directory data (firms, deadlines) is not yours and is unaffected. Consider exporting your data first.
